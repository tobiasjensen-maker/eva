How the work is built.

Four layers — and you only ever deal with the last one.

  1. 01

    Action

    One read or write against the ledger. Read a bank line, post an entry.

  2. 02

    Skill

    An ordered set of actions EVA can perform — reconcile a period, prepare a VAT return, run payroll.

  3. 03

    Routine

    Skills chained together with a trigger, the checks that apply, and how much autonomy they get.

  4. 04

    Run

    One execution of a routine against one agreement and one period. That is what lands in your review.

The fair questions

Will I lose control?

You approve everything that matters. Autonomy is capped per routine, EVA enforces the caps, and every action can be reversed.

Is it compliant and auditable?

Every action carries a full trace — inputs, reasoning, what changed. Export it whenever you need it.

Do we have to switch systems?

No. Agent Space is a layer built on top of e-conomic, working on the ledger you already keep.

Will it replace my staff?

It replaces the busywork, not the accountant. EVA takes the repetitive runs; your people become the advisors clients pay for.
