# EVA — Landing Page Messaging Brief

*Value proposition & USPs, oriented toward accounting offices (AOs). Raw material for a modern, high-conversion landing page.*

---

## 1. Positioning (the one-liner)

**EVA is the AI colleague that runs your practice's busywork — so your team can spend their time advising, not bookkeeping.**

Longer form:

> EVA is an agentic accounting assistant built into e-conomic. It doesn't just flag work — it does it: reconciliations, VAT, payroll, document chasing, month-end. It hands finished drafts back for your one-click approval, and gives you a single cockpit over every client, every task, and everyone in the office.

---

## 2. Who it's for

Accounting offices (AOs) managing dozens of client agreements — drowning in recurring compliance work, under margin and talent pressure, and trying to move up the value chain into advisory.

**Their real problem:** the work scales linearly with clients, but headcount can't. Overview lives in spreadsheets and people's heads.

---

## 3. Hero (headline + subhead options)

Pick the angle that fits the visual:

- **"Your practice, on autopilot — with you in the pilot's seat."**
  EVA does the recurring accounting work across all your clients and hands it back for approval. You keep full control and finally get your time back.

- **"Stop doing the books. Start running the practice."**
  One cockpit over every client task. An AI colleague that clears the busywork. The room to become the advisor your clients actually pay for.

- **"The operating system for your accounting office."**
  See everything, automate the routine, approve the rest — across every client and every accountant, in one place.

Primary CTA: **Book a demo** · Secondary: **See EVA in action** (product tour).

---

## 4. The core value proposition (3 pillars)

Structure the page around these three — they map to *see → do → trust*:

1. **One cockpit over the whole practice** — every client task, who owns it, when it's due, what's at risk. Switch between *My work* and *the whole office* in a click.
2. **EVA takes the busywork off your plate** — an agent that actually completes recurring tasks and routines across clients, then queues them for your sign-off.
3. **You never lose control** — every action is traceable, capped, and reversible. EVA drafts; you approve. Compliance-safe by design.

---

## 5. USPs (the differentiators — this is the meat)

| USP | The claim | Why it's different |
|---|---|---|
| **Agentic, not just automated** | EVA *does the task end-to-end* and hands back a draft — not another rule or alert. | Competitors surface work or automate rigid rules. EVA reasons through the task and finishes it. |
| **Whole-practice overview (Praksis)** | One place across every client *and* every accountant — workload, overdue, what EVA handled. | Most tools are per-client silos. This is the office's command centre. |
| **You stay in control** | See exactly what EVA did (full trace), set autonomy caps, approve, take over, or reverse. | Trust isn't a promise — it's built in. No black box. |
| **One model for tasks *and* routines** | Create work once, assign it to EVA or a person, one-off or recurring. No second tool. | A "routine" is just a task EVA does on repeat. One experience, not two. |
| **Frees the shift to advisory** | With the routine work handled, your people do the high-margin advisory. | Sells the outcome AOs are chasing, not just efficiency. |
| **Native to e-conomic** | Works where the books already live. Nothing to migrate, no new source of truth. | Zero switching cost; instant context on real ledger data. |

## 6. Section-by-section page copy (benefit-led)

**A · The overview problem** → *"Where's your practice actually at — right now?"*
Overdue VAT for three clients, a colleague underwater, a deadline nobody flagged. EVA shows it all on one screen, live — grouped by accountant, client, or deadline.

**B · EVA does the work** → *"Meet the colleague who never sleeps."*
Bank reconciliation, VAT returns, payroll runs, document chasing, month-end close. EVA handles them across your clients — you see what it's doing now, what it's scheduled to run tonight, and what's ready for your review.

**C · Review, don't redo** → *"One click from done."*
EVA drafts the work and shows you exactly what it did and the one thing that needs your judgement. Approve, or take it back — your call, in seconds.

**D · Set it once, run it everywhere** → *"Routines that scale with your client book."*
Turn any task into a routine and EVA runs it for every client it fits — with the autonomy limits you set. Extend it with connectors to the tools you already use.

**E · Be the advisor** → *"Move up the value chain."*
When the compliance work runs itself, your team's hours go where clients actually value them: advice, planning, growth.

**F · Trust layer** → *"Automation you can defend."*
Every action carries its provenance. Full audit trail on demand. Autonomy caps and a circuit-breaker keep EVA inside the lines. Nothing is irreversible.

## 8. Objection handling (put near the CTA)

- *"Will I lose control?"* → You approve everything that matters; autonomy is capped and reversible.
- *"Is it compliant/auditable?"* → Full trace on every action, no black box.
- *"Do I have to switch systems?"* → No — EVA lives inside e-conomic.
- *"Will it replace my staff?"* → It replaces the busywork, not the accountant. EVA is the colleague; your people become advisors.

---

## 9. Tone & visual direction (for "super modern")

- **Voice:** confident, calm, peer-to-peer. "Colleague," not "robot." Outcome-led ("get your time back"), not feature-led.
- **Look:** clean, light, lots of whitespace; a soft near-white canvas with the product's white cards floating; e-conomic orange as the accent, EVA's purple for the AI moments. Product screenshots of the Cockpit as the hero visual, with the "Ready for your review / EVA is handling / Scheduled" lanes on show — that's the "wow."
- **One idea per section, big type, generous spacing.** Let the product screenshots carry the proof.
